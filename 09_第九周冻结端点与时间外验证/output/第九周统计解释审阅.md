### Warnings

| Type | Detail | Affected |
|------|--------|----------|
| Causal identification | Funding rate is endogenous and there is no untreated same-hour control. This is an association and temporal transportability exercise, not a forecast or causal test. | All coefficients |
| Temporal drift | The holdout mean primary outcome is lower than the train-based dose model prediction; the dose model does not beat the hour+asset baseline. | Primary holdout |
| Time-of-day confounding | UTC 00/08/16 are scheduled funding slots; hour fixed effects reduce but do not identify a causal settlement effect. | All training models |
| Post-treatment placebo boundary | Week 8 +3h/+5h checks remain after treatment and are intentionally excluded as causal controls. | Link to Week 8 |
| Multiple testing | Three endpoints were examined in this frozen stage; no claim of a global confirmatory family beyond this stage is made. | Training estimates |
| OI endogeneity | OI may respond to the same information and market state as the outcome; the BTC OI coefficient is not a causal mechanism estimate. | BTC OI model |
| Non-random OI missingness | 45 BTC OI rows are missing in the late holdout period (2026-09-21 to 2026-10-06), plus one early row; the missingness is time-concentrated. | BTC OI model |
| Covariate shift | Mean absolute funding dose is about 0.446 bp in training and 0.565 bp in holdout; p75 shifts from about 0.629 bp to 0.826 bp. | Temporal holdout |
| Measurement timing | The event index contains realized historical funding rates; pre-settlement forecast observability is not established. | Dose models and holdout |
| External validity | Sample is one venue, BTC/ETH, and 181 dates. | All results |

### Fallacy Scan

- **Coverage**: 11/11 fallacy types checked

| Fallacy | Severity | Detail | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Simpson's paradox | CAUTION | Pooled Week 8 activity differences were concentrated at UTC 00; Week 9 controls hour but does not remove all schedule-specific dynamics. | Report hour-specific results and avoid universal slot claims. |
| Ecological fallacy | CAUTION | Asset-event aggregates cannot identify individual trader beliefs or behavior. | Keep inference at market-event level. |
| Berkson's paradox | NOTE | Selection is limited to liquid BTC/ETH and complete event windows; no outcome-dependent selection is established. | Document the instrument universe and missingness. |
| Collider bias | CAUTION | Conditioning on OI can induce bias if OI is a common effect of market information and trading outcomes. | Treat OI as a state proxy, not a clean control. |
| Base-rate neglect | NOTE | No classification or conditional-probability metric is used. | Not applicable. |
| Regression to the mean | NOTE | Events were scheduled rather than selected by extreme outcome values. | Do not create future subgroups after observing outcomes. |
| Survivorship bias | CAUTION | One venue and two liquid instruments exclude unavailable or less liquid contracts. | Limit generalization. |
| Look-elsewhere effect | CAUTION | The endpoint was frozen for this stage, but prior weeks explored several outcomes and windows. | Treat the overall project as exploratory until a preregistered holdout. |
| Garden of forking paths | CAUTION | The 70/30 split and model specification are fixed in the script but were chosen after prior exploratory work. | Freeze the next sample and model before new analysis. |
| Correlation != causation | CAUTION | Dose coefficients and holdout prediction do not establish that funding caused activity. | Use association/prediction language. |
| Reverse causality | CAUTION | Funding, OI, volatility, and news may respond to common contemporaneous information. | Seek exogenous variation or a credible control design before causal claims. |
