## Validation Summary

Week 10 has 1,080 BTC/ETH settlement events across 181 funding-event dates. All 5,400 event-anchor rows have both adjacent 5-minute bars. The event-level unit and date clustering are consistent with the construction, but the identifying comparison is not causal: settlement times are scheduled and there is no untreated observation at the same exact UTC hour and minute.

The originally emphasized contrast, real settlement minus the mean of `-60/-45/-30/-15`, is positive for log quote-volume jump (`0.3129`, date-cluster 95% CI `[0.2605, 0.3653]`, p `<0.001`) and range jump (`3.5686 bp`, CI `[2.3894, 4.7478]`, p `<0.001`). These are event-time differences, not settlement effects. The lead curve is not monotonic: the `-60m` jump is already high (`0.3831` for log quote volume and `5.64 bp` for range), while the `-45/-30/-15m` quote-volume jumps are `0.0726/0.1596/0.0560`.

The direct same-minute-phase comparison `0−(-60)` is much smaller for log quote volume: `0.0976`, date-cluster CI `[0.0340, 0.1613]`, p=`0.0028`, BH q=`0.0049` in the 12-comparison family of three main endpoints × four leads. Its range difference is `0.6137 bp` (CI `[-0.6237, 1.8511]`, p=`0.329`, q=`0.395`). The 7-day and 14-day circular moving-block intervals for the volume contrast are `[0.0374, 0.1580]` and `[0.0414, 0.1575]`, respectively. Since `0` and `-60` are both minute `:00`, but differ by UTC hour and the latter crosses the prior UTC date for midnight settlements, this contrast reduces minute-phase mismatch but does not remove hour, date, or event-time confounding. It was added after inspection of the lead curve and is a sensitivity audit, not an independent confirmation.

Week 11's non-settlement 5-minute jumps independently show strong clock-phase regularity: mean log quote-volume jumps are about `0.371` at minute `:00` and `0.195` at `:30`, versus about `0.053` at `:15` and `:45`; range jumps show the same pattern (`5.73`, `3.53`, `1.72`, and `1.38 bp`, respectively). The phase plot is descriptive, pooled across hours, and is not a same-slot untreated control. Slot-specific `0−(-60)` quote-volume differences are concentrated at UTC 00; BTC/ETH estimates at UTC 08 and 16 have intervals including zero. Slot splits are exploratory and do not establish a universal settlement response.

Other endpoints do not establish a liquidity or order-flow mechanism: the Amihud-like proxy's mean contrast is `-0.0199` (CI `[-0.0413, 0.0015]`, p=`0.068`) and taker imbalance is `0.0156` (CI `[-0.0108, 0.0420]`, p=`0.245`). The Amihud-like measure is a transformed OHLCV proxy, not quoted spread, depth, or execution cost.

## 11-Item Statistical Fallacy Scan

1. **Simpson's paradox:** Checked. The pooled phase-matched quote-volume contrast is positive, while slot estimates differ in sign and precision; this is evidence of heterogeneity, not enough to claim a formal Simpson reversal. Report pooled and slot results together.
2. **Ecological fallacy:** Checked. Event-asset averages cannot establish individual trader beliefs, irrationality, positions, or behavior.
3. **Berkson's paradox / selection:** Checked. The analysis is restricted to the available Binance BTC/ETH sample and valid 5-minute bars; all requested event anchors matched, but external-market representativeness is not established.
4. **Collider bias:** Checked. No regression adjustment is used in the main paired contrasts. Do not control for post-settlement variables as a remedy for missing untreated controls.
5. **Base-rate neglect:** Not applicable to the current mean-difference estimands; no classification or conditional-probability claim is made.
6. **Regression to the mean:** Checked. Events were not selected for extreme outcomes or pre-event values; no regression-to-mean design is claimed.
7. **Survivorship / missingness:** Checked. All 1,080 events and 5,400 requested anchors matched. This does not rule out coverage selection before the stored dataset begins.
8. **Look-elsewhere effect:** Checked. The real-minus-lead family now applies BH only to the three specified main endpoints × four leads (12 tests). Taker imbalance is left exploratory. Slot and minute-phase summaries are descriptive and should not be selectively presented as confirmatory tests.
9. **Garden of forking paths:** Present as a risk. The clock-phase diagnosis followed inspection of the Week 10 lead curve; it is transparently labeled a sensitivity audit, not an independent preregistered confirmation. Freeze the next test family before further analysis.
10. **Correlation is not causation:** Major limitation. Scheduled event timing, UTC hour, minute phase, market-wide news, and volatility can jointly produce the observed differences. Do not write that settlement caused price pressure.
11. **Reverse causality:** Checked. The local window is temporally ordered around scheduled settlement, but the design cannot distinguish settlement effects from anticipation, concurrent information, or market-state dynamics.

Fallacy scan coverage: **11/11**.

## Measurement and Inference Boundaries

- `jump_quote_volume_log` is the difference between adjacent 5-minute log quote volumes; it is not a price-return jump.
- `jump_range_bp` is a high-low range proxy, not a signed price impact.
- The Amihud-like variable is an OHLCV-based transformed proxy and cannot be called measured market liquidity.
- The `-60m` window for UTC 00 events crosses the UTC date boundary. Clustering remains by original funding-event date; a 7-day circular moving-block bootstrap is reported as a sequence-dependence sensitivity, but cross-boundary dependence is not eliminated by relabeling.
- The 95% intervals and p-values quantify sampling uncertainty under the stated clustering/bootstrap procedures. They do not address unmeasured clock-time confounding or identify a causal effect.
- No current dataset provides a same-exchange, same-hour, same-minute untreated settlement cell: Binance events recur at UTC 00/08/16. BTC/ETH and 00/08/16 strata are heterogeneity splits, not controls.

## Validation Verdict

**ANALYZED; computation reproduced, causal claim unsupported.** The defensible finding is that local activity differs by event-time anchor and clock phase, with notable UTC-slot heterogeneity. The study has not isolated a funding-settlement effect. To make a causal claim, the next data design must add a credible source of same-slot counterfactual variation or a defensible cross-market comparison; repeating the same fixed-hour event window will not solve the identification problem.
