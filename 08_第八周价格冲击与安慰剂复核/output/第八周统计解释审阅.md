### Warnings

| Type | Detail | Affected |
|------|--------|----------|
| Time-of-day confounding | Pooled turnover differences are concentrated at 00 UTC; 08 UTC estimates are near zero and 16 UTC estimates are less precise. Placebo anchors also occur at different UTC hours. | All pooled real-minus-placebo comparisons |
| Post-treatment comparison | The +3h and +5h anchors follow the real funding event, so they may contain persistent treatment dynamics and are not untreated controls. | All placebo contrasts |
| Serial dependence | Date-cluster and 7-day circular moving-block intervals broadly agree for volume/range; the +5h proxy interval differs on whether it includes zero. | Confidence intervals |
| Multiple testing | BH adjustment covers only this week's eight metric-by-offset tests, not all exploratory outcomes and models from the prior seven weeks. | All p/q values |
| Proxy validity | OHLCV data do not observe quoted spread, book depth, or realized execution costs. The log1p proxy is non-directional and not measured in price basis points after transformation. | Amihud-like endpoint |
| Scope | Results use Binance BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT; generalization to other venues, assets, or regimes is untested. | External validity |

### Fallacy Scan

- **Coverage**: 11/11 fallacy types checked

| Fallacy | Severity | Detail | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Simpson's paradox | CAUTION | The pooled positive volume contrast masks material hour heterogeneity and is concentrated at UTC 00h; no clean direction reversal is established, but the aggregate is not representative of all three slots. | Report pooled and hour-stratified estimates together; do not generalize the pooled average across funding slots. |
| Ecological fallacy | CAUTION | The unit is an asset-event/bar aggregate. The result cannot establish individual trader beliefs, irrationality, or position-level behavior. | Keep claims at the market-event level. |
| Berkson's paradox | NOTE | The sample is restricted to two liquid contracts and available event windows; no outcome-dependent filtering is evident, but selection limits generalization. | State the instrument and venue scope; inspect missingness before extending the sample. |
| Collider bias | NOTE | The week-eight paired contrast does not condition on an obvious common effect. Prior OI regressions are separate and OI may be endogenous. | Do not treat adding OI controls as automatically solving confounding. |
| Base-rate neglect | NOTE | No classification, diagnostic, or conditional-probability claim is made. | Not applicable to these continuous event outcomes. |
| Regression to the mean | NOTE | Scheduled event times were not selected because outcomes were extreme. | Avoid future subgroup selection based on extreme realized post-event outcomes. |
| Survivorship bias | CAUTION | There is no participant attrition, but restricting analysis to BTC/ETH and one exchange excludes delisted, illiquid, or unavailable contracts. | Limit external-validity claims and document the universe rule. |
| Look-elsewhere effect | CAUTION | Eight current-week comparisons are BH-adjusted, but earlier weeks explored multiple outcomes and specifications. | Treat the full project as exploratory; validate on a holdout sample with frozen endpoints. |
| Garden of forking paths | CAUTION | Anchor offsets and outcome definitions were refined during sequential analysis; there was no preregistration. | Freeze the next-stage estimand, window, and multiplicity family before new data analysis. |
| Correlation != causation | CAUTION | The study is observational, and +3h/+5h comparisons are post-event timing checks rather than untreated controls. | Use associational language; causal claims need a credible source of exogenous variation. |
| Reverse causality | CAUTION | Event timing precedes the measured bars, but common news, volatility, and trading responses can affect both funding conditions and post-event outcomes. | Establish the assignment mechanism and address concurrent information shocks before making directional claims. |
